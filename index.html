Bulk cleanup

For most cleanup, go to the Inventory tab and use the checkboxes on the left of each row instead — select just the filaments you mean to change (like high-traffic loose spools you don't track closely) and use the bulk actions that appear. That keeps rare or intentionally-tracked items untouched.
